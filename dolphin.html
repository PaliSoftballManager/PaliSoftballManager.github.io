Incoming Freshmen & Prospective Players

Pali Softball welcomes dedicated student-athletes who are ready to compete, improve, and represent Pacific Palisades High School with pride.

  • All incoming freshmen are encouraged to try out
  • Multi-sport athletes are welcome
  • Coachability and effort matter most
  • Commitment and team mindset are expected

2026 Tryout Information

Dates: February 10–12, 2026

Time: 3:00 PM – 5:30 PM

Location: Stadium By The Sea

Bring: Cleats, glove, bat, water bottle

Requirements: Must be academically eligible and complete athletic clearance paperwork.

Program Expectations

  • Attendance at all practices and games
  • Maintain academic eligibility
  • Respect teammates, coaches, and school
  • Commitment to improvement and team culture
